- Can work as individual or as agency (agency = business, can earn more, can hire subcontractors).

## Lecture 1 (part 3) — tools: ratings, badges, cover letter, Connects (start)
- Whole Upwork career is built around rating (JSS + reviews). Complaints from failed freelancers: "dependency on rating" — true, but logical.
- Implication for us: first contracts must end with 5★ + good private feedback; pick small, clear jobs we can over-deliver on (matches our "first reviews" filter).
- Good reviews → better/pricier jobs; bad → fewer jobs. Rating exists on any platform; only lever = do the work well.
- "Big competition" con: ~15M clients vs ~5M freelancers (her numbers) — demand exceeds supply.
- Ignore $3–5 freelancers and freeloader clients; real competitors = good-rated freelancers with normal rates. US clients expect to pay for quality.
- Startup costs: her spend first 3 months ≤ $30/month (Connects); month 1 earned $190.
- Pros: direct access to Western clients with B1–B2 English (translator OK, all written); USD income; no earning ceiling (individual → agency; example: dev from Dnipro, $1M solo in 8 yrs, agency later).
- Ukraine: income to FOP group 3 (only group allowing foreign economic activity), USD account.
- Badges: Rising Talent → Top Rated → Top Rated Plus (+ Expert-Vetted). She says Rising Talent is given at registration — CONFLICTS with current Help Center (needs invite or ≥4.8 rating + ≥$250/12 mo); lecture may be older. Trust Help Center.
- Algorithms watch new freelancers: are they serious / staying long-term.
- Our check: Connects spend today is high (several 13–26 Connect bids) vs her ≤$30/month — keep bids targeted.
- Clients tier by budget too; Upwork matches small-budget clients ($10–50 tasks) with beginners. Beginners' feed shows small jobs — take them to grow.
- Top Rated: 90+ days on site, $1,000 earned, JSS ≥90% → badge within ~1 week; feed then shows $100–200 jobs. Lose it if JSS <90.
- Top Rated Plus: +90 days at TR, JSS ≥90, $10k total, a "long-term contract" (hers ~2 months) → $500–1,000+ jobs, company managers hire TR+.
- Her 2024 ramp: started Jan, first contract Feb $192 (tiny hourly $10/h jobs, e.g. 40 min = $8), Apr $573 (repeat client), May $921 → $1k cumulative → Top Rated; then ~$800–1,200/mo, autumn 12h/day.
- Our takeaway: first 3–5 reviews from $20–100 jobs (incl. small hourly); big $600–2,400 bids with 0 reviews are low-odds Connect spend. Repeat clients matter.

## Lecture 1 (part 6) — "probation period", Connects, how clients find freelancers, profile boost
- Rating level decides which clients you can get, but beginners DO have work: small-budget clients.
- Why freelancers fail: don't understand the algorithm and quit early. Need min 3 months (90 days) of activity on the site with relevant jobs to "break the wall" to normal clients. She calls it a probation period (like 3-month trial at a full-time job): Upwork checks if you're reliable before trusting rich clients ($1,000/video etc.).
- Results visible after ~2 months; commit to ≥3 months. Upwork is not "money right now".
- Connects = Upwork currency to apply. 100 Connects ≈ $15 + VAT (~$18). One job costs ~7–20 Connects; on average ~$1.5 per application (range $0.5–2).
- Why Connects exist: mass spam (copy-paste proposals, 50 junk proposals per job, scams) from low-cost countries; Connects stopped the flood → less low-quality competition for us.
- Her rate: ~5 applications per day to win 1 job ≈ $7–10 per won job, and that client can become long-term.
- Two ways clients find freelancers:
  1) Client posts a job → freelancers apply (pay Connects) → client messages ~3 of them → hires 1–2.
  2) Client searches talent manually (Search → Talent, e.g. "AI Video Editing"), filters by badge (Rising Talent green, Top Rated blue, Top Rated Plus pink), rate (many Rising Talent ≤ $10/h), location. Normal US clients prefer Western freelancers (mindset, comfort).
- Invite to interview from client = NO Connects to reply. Client can also just send a direct message ("are you free?") → no Connects.
- Goal: be at the top of talent search. Way 1: Boost Your Profile (pay Connects; auction like Google Ads, you set the bid e.g. 5–10; Connects charged ONLY when a client clicks your profile). She recommends it at the start (hers is off now). Way 2: (next chunk).
- Our status: "Open for work"/availability badge boost on, max 14 Connects/week, review Nov 19 — matches her advice.

## Lecture 1 (part 7) — organic ranking: activity rules, subscription
- Way 2 to rank in talent search: organic, like Google. Better profile + more activity → algorithm lifts you. Clients search manually a LOT.
- She says: do all of these or don't expect results.
  1) Log in EVERY day (at least every other day). Even just open site/mobile app so algorithm sees you online. Install Upwork mobile app.
  2) Reply to messages/invites within 1 hour, ideally 30 min (old freelancers' advice). Profile shows response time (e.g. "<24h" — too slow). If you can't talk now, at least reply "thanks, it's night here, I'll answer tomorrow at X". Best: start dialog, show portfolio, close the contract fast.
  3) Apply to jobs daily while you have no project / after a contract ends. Her + Telegram channels stats: 5–7 proposals/day → ~half reply (3–4) → ~1 contract. Clients may reply in 1–4 days, so apply every day.
  4) Apply in US working hours: ET/CT/PT zones start ~16:00–17:00 Kyiv. She monitored the feed 2–4 h from 16:00, chatted with clients until 23:00–00:00, did the work next morning. Some hours have no good jobs — keep scrolling.
  5) Change something in profile at least weekly (a word, dash, bracket, a paragraph) — rumor that algorithm treats it as update; she does it "just in case".
  6) Buy Freelancer Plus subscription for first 3 months: $20/month incl. 100 Connects (vs ~$18 for 100 Connects alone) + extra features; signals seriousness → organic boost. She spent $20 sub + ~$10 extra Connects/month. After Top Rated she dropped it and bought ~50 Connects when a contract ended.

- Our gaps: Freelancer Plus? (not noted — ask Bohdan). Mobile app with push for fast replies? Weekly profile micro-edit (can add to routine). Peak US hours 16:00–00:00 Kyiv already covered by checks.

## Lecture 1 (part 8) — profile setup (her profile as example)
- Her profile is not "the standard"; also study top freelancers in YOUR niche (high rating, good earnings, from Europe/US).
- Do NOT write the profile with ChatGPT: clients spot it; GPT doesn't know insider Upwork experience. Write yourself in Ukrainian, use GPT only to translate / for theses.
- Photo: face fills ~3/4 of the circle (client can't zoom), smiling, ideally with teeth. US clients buy from smiling positive people.
- Header: name, location (auto), JSS, rating.
- "Available Now" badge: turn ON; costs 1 Connect/day; some clients filter by it — without it you're excluded.
- 1 General profile + 2 specialized sub-profiles (hers: Video Editor, AI Video Creator). When applying choose the matching sub-profile. Use it — Upwork sees you use its tools.
- Title (profile + each sub-profile): put search keywords clients type (e.g. "AI video", "Premiere", popular AI tools). Character-limited → keywords + profession. Check titles of top freelancers in niche.
- Rate: start LOW = minimum of your niche (she started $10/h at Rising Talent), because beginner-level clients have small budgets. Not dumping ($5–9). Raising later is just editing the number: Top Rated → $15, busy → $18, TR+ → $20, added AI → $30. Analogy: top manicure master vs regular.
- Overview rules: client compares 10–20 freelancers, no time → long texts/portfolios annoy and get skipped. Be laconic, save client's time.
  - First line = what you do for whom ("I create videos for businesses with AI and Adobe"). No "Hello, my name is…" (already shown).
  - General skills block (years of experience, main skills).
  - Education (if relevant; if not matching profession, still state it).
  - Soft skills: short sentences 5–7 words, in blocks; client scans diagonally and grabs keywords.
  - Tools: list main popular ones only, not everything.
  - "Why work with me": one short paragraph max.
  - Hobbies line (drawing, running, hiking, reading) → makes you a living person (marketing).
  - END with call to action, never "I'd be happy to work with you". CTA = tell them what to do ("Contact me to discuss…", "Message me for an interview") OR a question ("Could you tell me more about your business?", "What are the next steps?"). Use CTA at the end of every selling message/proposal.
- Video intro: mandatory. Not a slideshow of works — film YOURSELF. Purpose: show what kind of person you are, that it's comfortable to work with you. 40–50 sec max. Smiling, neat (clean ironed shirt/T-shirt), good background, light on face, energetic positive voice. If English weak: ask GPT for a 40–50s script at B1/B2 level, read from laptop 1–2 sentences at a time with pauses, then cut together. Monotone "Hello, I'm from Ukraine, I have experience" won't work — "play the role".
- Our check vs this: Available badge ON ✅. Sub-profiles: unknown — check. Rate $20/h vs her "start at niche minimum" — consider $15 for first reviews (dev niche min is higher than video). Overview: currently pain-first; check for short lines, hobbies line, CTA at end. Video: reused Fiverr video — check it shows Bohdan smiling on camera, ≤50s. Photo: check 3/4 face + smile.
